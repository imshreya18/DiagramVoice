Tool								What it does				Gap for your user
Be My Eyes / Be My AI						Describes any photo with AI		General-purpose. It isn't built for exam-style graphs and circuits, has no structured output, no Hindi STEM tutoring, and no tactile output

How yours is different
Works on any photo or PDF page from the student's own textbook, not just pre-made content.
Two-step pipeline: it extracts structured data first, then explains. That makes it more grounded than "describe this image."
Honest about uncertainty. It flags unreadable values and says "approximately," and it says "I'm not sure" instead of inventing numbers.
Voice in, voice out, in Hindi and English, with a tutor-style answer to concept doubts.
Free and web-based, so no app install, no special hardware, and it works on a phone.
